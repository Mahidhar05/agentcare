CARDIOLOGY DEPARTMENT — AGENTCARE HOSPITAL

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT:
Our Cardiology Department is a state-of-the-art facility 
specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of heart and 
cardiovascular diseases. We combine advanced technology 
with compassionate care.

SERVICES OFFERED:

Diagnostic Services:
- Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG)
- Echocardiogram (2D, 3D, and 4D)
- Stress Test (Treadmill Test)
- Holter Monitoring (24-72 hours)
- Cardiac MRI and CT
- Angiography
- Coronary Calcium Scoring

Interventional Procedures:
- Coronary Angioplasty (PTCA)
- Stent Placement
- Balloon Valvuloplasty
- Pacemaker Implantation
- ICD Implantation
- Cardiac Ablation
- Structural Heart Interventions (TAVR, MitraClip)

Surgical Procedures:
- Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)
- Valve Repair/Replacement
- Heart Transplant (Reference)
- Congenital Heart Surgery

Cardiac Rehabilitation:
- Post-surgery recovery program
- Exercise therapy
- Nutritional counseling
- Stress management

WHEN TO CONSULT A CARDIOLOGIST:
- Chest pain or discomfort
- Shortness of breath
- Rapid or irregular heartbeat
- Dizziness or fainting
- Swelling in legs, ankles, or feet
- Family history of heart disease
- High blood pressure or cholesterol
- Diabetes
- Age 40+ for routine checkup
