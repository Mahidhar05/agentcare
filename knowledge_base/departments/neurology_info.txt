NEUROLOGY DEPARTMENT — AGENTCARE HOSPITAL

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT:
The Neurology Department specializes in the diagnosis and 
treatment of disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, 
nerves, and muscles. Our team provides comprehensive care 
for a wide range of neurological conditions.

SERVICES OFFERED:

Diagnostic Services:
- Electroencephalogram (EEG)
- Electromyography (EMG)
- Nerve Conduction Studies (NCS)
- Sleep Studies (Polysomnography)
- Brain MRI and CT
- Cerebral Angiography
- Lumbar Puncture

Treatment Services:
- Stroke Management
- Epilepsy Treatment
- Migraine Management
- Movement Disorders Clinic
- Memory Clinic
- Multiple Sclerosis Clinic
- Neuromuscular Disorders Care

Interventional Neurology:
- Thrombectomy for Stroke
- Cerebral Aneurysm Coiling
- Carotid Stenting
- Deep Brain Stimulation

CONDITIONS TREATED:

WHEN TO CONSULT A NEUROLOGIST:
- Frequent or severe headaches
- Seizures or loss of consciousness
- Sudden weakness or numbness
- Memory problems
- Difficulty speaking or understanding
- Vision problems
- Balance issues or dizziness
- Tremors or involuntary movements
- Sleep disorders
- Chronic back or neck pain
