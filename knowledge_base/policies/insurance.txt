Private Insurance:
- Star Health & Allied Insurance
- HDFC ERGO General Insurance
- ICICI Lombard General Insurance
- Bajaj Allianz General Insurance
- New India Assurance
- Oriental Insurance
- Care Health Insurance

- Religare Health Insurance
- Manipal Cigna Health Insurance

Corporate Insurance:
- Most corporate group health insurance policies accepted
- TPA (Third Party Administrator) support: MediAssist, FHPL, Vidal, Paramount

Government Schemes:
- Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
- CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme)
- ESIC (Employee State Insurance Corporation)
- State government insurance schemes

CASHLESS TREATMENT:
- Available for empanelled insurance providers
- Pre-authorization required 24 hours before planned procedures
- For emergencies, notify within 24 hours of admission
- Present insurance card and government ID at admission

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Original insurance card
- Government-issued photo ID (Aadhar/Passport/PAN)
- Latest insurance policy document
- Corporate letter (for group policies)
- Referral letter (if applicable)

CLAIM PROCESS:
1. Register at Insurance Help Desk on Ground Floor
2. Submit required documents
3. Pre-authorization sent to insurance company
4. Approval typically within 2-4 hours
5. Balance amount (if any) settled at discharge
