AGENTCARE HOSPITAL — VISITING HOURS POLICY

GENERAL VISITING HOURS:
- Monday to Sunday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
- Maximum 2 visitors per patient at a time
- Children under 12 not permitted in patient rooms

ICU VISITING HOURS:
- Monday to Sunday: 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM
- Only 2 visitors at a time, one at a time in the room
- No children under 16 permitted

MATERNITY WARD:
- Monday to Sunday: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM (extended hours)
- Fathers/partners: 24 hours access
- Siblings allowed 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM

PEDIATRIC WARD:
- Parents: 24 hours access
- Other visitors: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Please avoid bringing sick individuals

EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT:
- Visitor policy at discretion of medical staff
- Generally, 1 attendant allowed with patient

RULES FOR ALL VISITORS:
- Please maintain silence in wards
- Turn off mobile phones or use silent mode
- No flowers in ICU
- No outside food in patient areas
- Wear provided visitor badge at all times
- Comply with any hospital dress code
