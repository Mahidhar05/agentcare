BLOOD TEST PREPARATION GUIDE

TYPES OF BLOOD TESTS:

1. Fasting Blood Tests
Requires 8-12 hours of fasting before blood draw
Common tests:
- Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
- Lipid Profile (cholesterol test)
- Liver Function Test (LFT)
- Kidney Function Test (KFT)
- Iron studies
- Homocysteine

Fasting Instructions:
- No food or drinks (except water) for 8-12 hours
- Best to schedule test in morning
- Continue prescription medications with water unless instructed otherwise
- No coffee, tea, juice, or milk during fasting

2. Non-Fasting Blood Tests
No fasting required:
- Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- HbA1c (diabetes control test)
- Thyroid Function Test (TFT)
- Hormone tests
- Vitamin levels
- Infection screening

3. Post-Prandial Blood Tests
Test done 2 hours after a meal:
- Post-Prandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)
- Post-meal insulin

BEFORE YOUR TEST:

24 HOURS BEFORE:
- Avoid excessive alcohol (24 hours)
- Avoid heavy exercise (24 hours)
- Get adequate sleep
- Stay well hydrated (water only for fasting tests)

DAY OF TEST:
- Arrive 15 minutes early for check-in
- Bring:
  * Doctor's prescription/request form
  * Government-issued photo ID
  * Insurance card (if applicable)
  * List of current medications

Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that can be rolled up easily.

DURING BLOOD DRAW:
1. Technician will clean your arm with antiseptic
2. Tourniquet placed on upper arm
3. Needle inserted in vein (usually inner elbow)
4. Blood collected in tubes (2-15 ml typically)
5. Pressure applied to stop bleeding
6. Bandage or plaster applied

Total time: 5-10 minutes

AFTER YOUR TEST:
- Keep bandage on for 1 hour
- Avoid heavy lifting with that arm for a few hours
- Drink water and eat if you were fasting
- Minor bruising is normal

FEELING FAINT?
- Some people feel dizzy during blood draw
- Inform technician immediately
- You'll be asked to lie down
- Recovery usually within minutes

RESULTS:
- Routine tests: 24-48 hours
- Urgent tests: 2-4 hours
- Available in patient portal or via email
- Discuss with your doctor for interpretation
