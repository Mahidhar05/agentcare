MRI SCAN PREPARATION GUIDE

WHAT IS AN MRI?
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) uses powerful magnets and radio waves 
to create detailed images of organs, tissues, and structures inside the body.

BEFORE YOUR MRI:

24 HOURS BEFORE:
- Continue your normal diet and medications unless instructed otherwise
- If contrast dye will be used, you may need to fast for 4 hours before scan

DAY OF SCAN:

Fasting Requirements:
- MRI without contrast: No fasting required
- MRI with contrast: Fast for 4 hours before scan (no food or drink)
- Diabetic patients: Contact your doctor for special instructions

Clothing:
- Wear comfortable, loose clothing without metal
- You may need to change into a hospital gown
- Avoid clothing with zippers, buttons, or metal fasteners

Metal Items — REMOVE THESE:
- Jewelry (rings, earrings, necklaces, watches)
- Body piercings
- Hair pins, clips
- Hearing aids
- Removable dental work
- Wigs (some contain metal)
- Belts, coins, keys
- Credit cards, phones (magnetic strips will be erased)

Makeup and Skincare:
- Remove all makeup (some contain metal particles)
- Do not use hair spray or gel
- Do not apply moisturizer or perfume

IMPORTANT MEDICAL INFORMATION TO SHARE:
- Pregnancy or possibility of pregnancy
- Metal implants (pacemakers, cochlear implants, aneurysm clips)
- Metal fragments in body (from injury or work)
- Recent surgery
- Kidney problems (if contrast is used)
- Allergies (especially to contrast dye)
- Claustrophobia

WHAT TO EXPECT:

Duration: 30-90 minutes depending on area being scanned

The Scan:
1. You'll lie on a table that slides into the MRI machine
2. The machine makes loud tapping/knocking sounds (earplugs provided)
3. You must remain very still for image quality
4. Technician can communicate with you throughout

If Contrast is Used:
- Small IV inserted in arm
- Contrast dye injected during scan
- May cause temporary metallic taste or warm sensation

AFTER YOUR MRI:
- Resume normal activities immediately
- Drink extra water if contrast was used (flushes from system)
- Results available in 24-48 hours
- Report will be sent to your doctor

CLAUSTROPHOBIC?
- Inform staff before scan
- Mild sedation available if pre-arranged
- Some facilities offer open MRI (less enclosed)
