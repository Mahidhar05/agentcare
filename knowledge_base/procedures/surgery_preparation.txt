SURGERY PREPARATION GUIDE

BEFORE YOUR SURGERY:

2 WEEKS BEFORE:
- Complete all pre-operative tests as prescribed
- Meet with your surgeon for pre-surgical consultation
- Review all medications with your doctor
- Stop smoking (highly recommended for healing)
- Reduce alcohol consumption

1 WEEK BEFORE:
- Continue prescribed antibiotics if given
- Avoid blood-thinning medications (aspirin, ibuprofen) if advised
- Arrange for someone to drive you home post-surgery
- Arrange for help at home for a few days
- Prepare recovery area at home

3 DAYS BEFORE:
- Complete any pending pre-op tests
- Practice deep breathing exercises
- Follow prescribed diet if any
- Ensure adequate rest and sleep

24 HOURS BEFORE:
- Fasting: No solid food after midnight
- Clear liquids allowed up to 2 hours before surgery
- Take shower with antibacterial soap
- Wash hair (may not be able to for days after)
- Get good night's sleep

DAY OF SURGERY:

BEFORE ARRIVING:
- Shower with antibacterial soap
- Brush teeth (do not swallow water)
- Do NOT eat or drink anything (unless instructed)
- Take only medications approved by anesthesiologist
- Remove all makeup, nail polish, jewelry
- Wear comfortable, loose clothing

WHAT TO BRING:
- Government-issued photo ID
- Insurance card and pre-authorization
- List of current medications
- Emergency contact information
- Comfortable clothes for going home
- Personal toiletries (if staying overnight)
- Book or entertainment for waiting
- DO NOT bring valuables or jewelry

AT THE HOSPITAL:
1. Check-in at reception (arrive 2 hours before scheduled surgery)
2. Complete admission paperwork
3. Change into hospital gown
4. Medical team will review your case
5. Anesthesiologist will meet with you
6. IV line will be placed
7. Surgeon will visit before procedure

ANESTHESIA TYPES:
- General Anesthesia: You'll be completely asleep
- Regional Anesthesia: Numbs a specific area (spinal, epidural)
- Local Anesthesia: Numbs small area, you're awake
- Conscious Sedation: Relaxed but awake

AFTER SURGERY:

RECOVERY ROOM:
- Monitored for 1-2 hours after surgery
- Vital signs checked frequently
- Pain management started
- Nausea may be treated

RETURN TO WARD/HOME:
- Follow all discharge instructions
- Take pain medication as prescribed
- Keep surgical area clean and dry
- Watch for signs of infection

WHEN TO CALL DOCTOR:
- Fever above 101°F (38.3°C)
- Excessive bleeding or drainage
- Increasing pain not relieved by medication
- Redness, warmth, or swelling at surgical site
- Difficulty breathing
- Nausea/vomiting for more than 24 hours

FOLLOW-UP:
- First follow-up: Usually 7-14 days post-surgery
- Bring surgical site care instructions
- List any questions or concerns
